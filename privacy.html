Privacy Policy

Applies to openwarintelligence.org, the installable web app (PWA) and the Android/iOS apps that wrap it. Version of 8 November 2026.

OpenWar Intelligence is a free, open-source (MIT) dashboard that shows public open-source data about military and geopolitical events. It has no user accounts and sells nothing. This page says, plainly, what is collected and by whom.

1. Who is responsible

The site and the apps are published by Cappa Business S.r.l., Via Bagutta 13, 20121 Milano, Italy. Contact for privacy matters: openwarintelligence@gmail.com.

3. Third-party services the dashboard contacts

The dashboard runs in your browser and fetches public data directly. Each of these services receives your IP address and the request as any website would. None receives your name.

We are working to remove the public CORS proxies and any source whose terms do not allow this use; the current list is kept honest in the documentation.

4. Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish visitors. You can block them with your browser or an extension; the dashboard works without them.

5. Apps from the stores

The Android and iOS apps are wrappers around this website. They load only openwarintelligence.org and the services above, collect nothing on their own, request no permissions beyond network access and send no notifications unless you opt in to a future notification feature, which will be described here before it is released.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask for access, correction, deletion or export of the data you gave us through the registration form, and withdraw newsletter consent at any time, by writing to openwarintelligence@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

7. Changes

Changes to this page are dated at the top and recorded in the project's public repository.
